01 / COMMUNITY RULES

OFFICIAL ESS RULES

FOLLOW THESE RULES

Discord & Minecraft

All rules are enforced on Discord and Minecraft.

Discord Terms ↗ Discord Guidelines ↗ Minecraft Community Standards ↗ View Official ESS Rules PDF ↗
  • Follow the Discord rules and Minecraft rules.
  • No hacking of any kind.
  • No abusing bugs or exploiting items without permission from the server owner or Mine. Exploited items are especially prohibited in videos.
  • No player disrespect, harassment, excessive toxicity, or dark or offensive jokes.
  • No excessive caps, spam, flooding chat, or excessive pinging.
  • Do not disobey staff or troll or create joke tickets.
  • No disruptive sound, music, or noise in voice calls.
  • No self-promotion or advertising unless approved for a partnership.
  • Use common sense and do not break the rules.
  • No usernames or skins that match any character in the East Stable SMP.
  • Do not post screenshots or videos of events before the ESS creators publish theirs.
  • Do not join event servers unless invited by an event channel or approved by the owner, co-owner, or an operator.
  • Trying to join secret events can result in bans.
  • Do not paste builds in without asking.
  • No racism, sexism, homophobia, or discrimination of any kind.
  • No DDoS, doxxing, leaking personal information, threats, or swatting.
  • No NSFW or 18+ sites, links, art, photos, or content.
  • Swearing is allowed, but slurs are never allowed.
  • Do not use weapons you are not permitted to use. Doing so may result in a 2-day ban or 1-video ban.
  • No attempting to pressure East to change pings.
  • No spam, advertising, doxxing, threats, disrespect, harassment, excessive toxicity, NSFW content, racism, homophobia, religion or politics discussions, excessive pinging, or troll/joke tickets.
  • No posting religion or politics topics.
  • By using this Discord server, you represent and warrant that you have reached the age of majority in your jurisdiction and have the full right and authority to consent to these rules.
  • All permanent punishments are final and not appealable.

NOTICE OF RECORDING

Consent & Likeness

You are aware and agree that audio and video recording may occur in the Servers. By continuing to use the Servers you:

  1. A. Consent to being recorded and grant other users and their entities an irrevocable, non-exclusive license to use, edit, and record your in-game name or alias, image, voice, and likeness in connection with content creation for any purpose, in perpetuity, without royalty or attribution. Recording users are not required to include or retain unique identifiers such as usernames or account references.
  2. B. Waive and release claims related to use of your likeness or recorded media, including claims for invasion of privacy, violation of publicity rights, defamation, copyright infringement, or fees for use.
  3. C. Acknowledge that you have read this notice and understand that recording will rely on this consent and release, which is binding upon your successors, heirs, and assigns.

Your username is automatically collected each time you use the Servers. This information is used only to verify consent, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce these rules. You may contact a Server Operator to access, correct, or delete this information. Information-related requests will be recorded.

These Server Participation Rules may be amended at any time. For questions, contact a Server Operator through the Discord server.
